# 产品信息：Arcos Chair

## 设计师

Lievore Altherr

## 设计说明

Chair with sled base and integrated armrests made of powder-coated aluminum. Shell in metal and plywood is upholstered in fabric, leather, coated fabric or customer's own material. The upholstery can be separated and replaced by technical personnel, since it has been applied without the use of glue. A version with removable cover is available upon request with a surcharge.
